Free Chess Game Review

Unlimited game analysis with Stockfish 19, right in your browser. Load your last 20 games from Chess.com or Lichess by username, or paste any PGN, and see every move rated.

How it works

1. Pick your siteChess.com, Lichess, or paste a PGN from anywhere.
2. Get your gamesType your username once. Your last 20 games appear, and next time they load by themselves.
3. ReviewTap a game. Every move is rated, with the better move you missed and a graph of the whole game.

What the review shows

Each move gets a clear label: Best, Excellent, Good, Inaccuracy, Mistake, Miss or Blunder. For every mistake you see the engine's better move as an arrow on the board, plus a short explanation. The evaluation graph shows where the game turned, and the summary counts your best moves and blunders, so you know exactly what to work on.

Why it is free and unlimited

Most sites limit free game reviews because the analysis runs on their servers. Here the Stockfish 19 engine runs on your own computer or phone, inside the browser, so there is nothing to limit. No sign-up, no daily cap.

Questions

How do I review my Chess.com games?

Choose Chess.com, type your username and press Get My Games. Tap any of your last 20 games to review it. Your username is remembered in your browser.

Can I review Lichess games or any PGN?

Yes. Choose Lichess and enter your Lichess username, or choose Paste PGN and paste a game from any chess site or book.

Do you store my games or my username?

No. Your games are read straight from Chess.com or Lichess by your browser, and your username stays in your own browser. Nothing is sent to us.
