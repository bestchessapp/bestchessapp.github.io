Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is BEST CHESS really free?

Yes. Playing, game reviews, all puzzles, Chess Basics and the first lessons of every course are free for ever, with no daily limits and no ads. BEST CHESS Pro is an optional one-time purchase that unlocks every Chess Academy lesson.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything runs on your phone, including the chess engine. You need no internet, no account and no sign-up.

Can I review games I played on other sites?

Yes. Copy the game's PGN from any chess site, paste it into BEST CHESS (or open a PGN file) and get a full review: move labels, accuracy, the best line and an evaluation graph.

Which chess engine does it use?

Stockfish 15.1, one of the strongest open-source chess engines, running on your phone as a separate program under the GNU GPL v3. Engine source code.

Does BEST CHESS collect my data?

No. The app has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no internet permission. Your games and progress stay on your phone. Read the privacy policy.

Is it good for beginners?

Yes. "New to Chess?" starts with every rule step by step, and the Chess Academy grows with you, up to openings, strategy and endgames. The computer's easiest level plays like a real beginner.
