Italian Game

1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Bc4

The Italian Game is one of the oldest openings in chess and still played at every level. After 1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Bc4, White's bishop aims at f7, the weak point next to Black's king.

The calm main line, with c3 and d3, builds a solid centre, castles quickly and keeps every option open for the middlegame. It follows the three opening rules: control the centre, develop your pieces and castle.

The idea

  • e4 and Nf3 take the centre and attack e5.
  • Bc4 develops the bishop to its most active square, aiming at f7.
  • c3 prepares d4; d3 keeps e4 protected; castling makes the king safe.

After the moves

The Italian Game: quick development and a safe king.

How to spot it in your games

  • Watch f7: many Italian tactics hit that square.
  • Do not move the same piece twice in the opening without a good reason.
  • Once castled, look to play d4 or a slow plan on the queenside.
