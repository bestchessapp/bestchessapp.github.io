Queen's Gambit

1.d4 d5 2.c4

The Queen's Gambit begins 1.d4 d5 2.c4. White offers the c-pawn to pull Black's d-pawn away from the centre. It is not a real sacrifice: if Black takes, White usually wins the pawn back.

In the Queen's Gambit Declined, Black keeps the centre with 2...e6. White develops with Nc3, Bg5 and e3, pins the knight that defends d5 and prepares to castle.

The idea

  • c4 challenges Black's d5 pawn from the side.
  • Nc3 and Bg5 add pressure on d5 and the knight that defends it.
  • e3 opens the way for the light-squared bishop; castling comes next.

After the moves

The Queen's Gambit: steady pressure on the centre.

How to spot it in your games

  • Count attackers and defenders on d5: that square is the heart of the opening.
  • A pin on the f6 knight weakens Black's control of the centre.
  • Finish development before starting an attack.
