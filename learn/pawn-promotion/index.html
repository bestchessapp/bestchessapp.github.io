Chess Rules

Pawn Promotion

A pawn that reaches the end becomes a new piece

The rule

When a pawn reaches the last rank (the eighth for White, the first for Black), it must be exchanged for a queen, rook, bishop or knight of the same colour. It cannot stay a pawn and cannot become a king.

You may promote to a piece you still have, so it is possible to have two or more queens.

One step from promotion: e8=Q gives White a new queen.

Almost always a queen

The queen is the strongest piece, so players promote to a queen in nearly every game.

Choosing another piece is called underpromotion. A knight can be better when it gives check or mate at once, and a rook or bishop can avoid stalemate in some endgames.

How it is written

Promotion is written with the new piece after an equals sign: e8=Q, or exd8=N for a capture that promotes to a knight.
