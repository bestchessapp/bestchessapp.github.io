Fool's Mate

The Fastest Checkmate

Fool's mate is the shortest checkmate possible in chess: Black mates on the second move. It only happens if White weakens the king with two careless pawn moves, f3 and g4, opening the diagonal from h4 to e1.

Nobody loses this way against a careful opponent, but it teaches a lesson that matters in every game: pawn moves in front of your king can open lines that never close again.

The idea

  • The e1–h4 diagonal leads straight to White's king.
  • f3 and g4 remove both pawns that guard it.
  • The black queen checks from h4, and nothing can block or capture.

After the moves

Fool's mate. Keep the pawns in front of your king.

How to spot it in your games

  • Be careful with f- and g-pawn moves while your king is still in the centre.
  • If an opponent opens a diagonal to their king, look for a queen check along it.
  • Develop pieces before making pawn moves near your own king.
