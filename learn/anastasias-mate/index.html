Anastasia's Mate

Knight and Rook on the Edge

Anastasia's mate takes its name from a novel of 1803 in which it appears. A knight takes away the king's escape squares, and a rook or queen mates along the edge of the board, while the king's own pawn blocks the last square.

In games it usually comes after a queen sacrifice: the queen captures next to the king, the king is forced to take, and the rook arrives with mate on the open file.

The idea

  • The king is on the edge (often h7 or h8), with its own pawn beside it.
  • A knight on e7 (or the mirror square) guards the escape squares.
  • The rook mates along the open file, usually the h-file.

After the moves

The queen opened the h-file for the rook.

How to spot it in your games

  • A knight deep in the enemy camp near a castled king is a strong hint.
  • If the h-file is closed, a queen sacrifice on h7 can open it.
  • Check that the king has no escape: knight, pawn and rook must cover every square.
