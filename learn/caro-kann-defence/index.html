Caro-Kann Defence

Solid as a Rock

The Caro-Kann Defence, 1.e4 c6, is a safe and sturdy answer to 1.e4. Black prepares d5 to challenge the centre at once, while keeping the light-squared bishop free.

In the main line Black trades in the centre and brings the bishop out to f5 before playing e6. The result is a solid position with no weaknesses and a clear plan.

The idea

  • c6 prepares d5 without blocking the c8 bishop.
  • After the trade in the centre, Bf5 develops the bishop before e6.
  • Bg6 keeps the bishop on its good diagonal when it is attacked.

After the moves

The Caro-Kann: no weaknesses and every piece has a future.

How to spot it in your games

  • Develop the light bishop before playing e6, or it will be shut in.
  • Answer threats to the bishop by retreating along the same diagonal.
  • Aim for Nf6, e6 and castling: a calm, healthy position.
