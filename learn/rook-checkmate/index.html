How to Checkmate with a Rook

The Ladder

A king and a rook can checkmate a lone king too. The rook cuts the enemy king off along a rank or file, your king walks up in front of it, and the rook gives mate on the edge.

With two rooks it is even easier: the 'ladder'. One rook cuts off a rank, the other checks on the next one, and they climb step by step until the king reaches the edge.

The idea

  • The rook builds a wall the king cannot cross.
  • Kings facing each other leave the rook free to check on the edge.
  • Two rooks take turns: one cuts off, one checks.

After the moves

The ladder mate: each rook holds a rank.

How to spot it in your games

  • Keep the rook far from the enemy king so it cannot be attacked.
  • Bring your king up until the kings stand opposite each other.
  • In the ladder, move the rook that is not giving check to the next rank.
