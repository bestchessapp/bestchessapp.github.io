How to Checkmate with a King and Queen

King and Queen Win

A king and a queen can always checkmate a lone king. The plan has three steps: use the queen to push the enemy king to the edge of the board, bring your own king close, then give mate.

The only danger is stalemate: if the enemy king has no legal move but is not in check, the game is a draw. Always leave the king at least one square until the mating move.

The idea

  • The queen restricts the king like a box that gets smaller.
  • Your king must help: the queen alone cannot mate.
  • Mate happens on the edge, with your king in front of the enemy king.

How to spot it in your games

  • Keep the queen a knight's move away from the enemy king to shrink its box safely.
  • Before each queen move, check that the enemy king still has a move (no stalemate).
  • When the kings face each other, look for the queen check on the edge.
