Checkmate vs Stalemate

One wins the game, the other is a draw

Checkmate

Checkmate means the king is in check and has no legal way to escape: it cannot move to a safe square, the check cannot be blocked, and the attacking piece cannot be captured. The game ends and the side that gave checkmate wins.

Stalemate

Stalemate means the player to move is not in check but has no legal move at all. The game ends immediately in a draw, even if the other side has a huge material advantage.

How to avoid stalemate when winning

Before each move in a winning endgame, ask: after my move, does my opponent still have a legal move? If the answer is no and the king is not in check, it is stalemate.

Keep the enemy king one square of room until the moment you can give mate.
