Chess Clock

A free chess timer for over-the-board games. Choose a time, put the phone between the players, and tap your side after each move.

10:00

10:00

Tip: Black taps first to start White's clock. Space bar switches sides on a computer. The screen stays on while the clock runs (on browsers that allow it).

How to use a chess clock

A chess clock has two timers, one for each player. After you make your move, press your side of the clock: your time stops and your opponent's time starts. If your time reaches zero you lose on time, unless your opponent has no way at all to checkmate, in which case the game is a draw.

Common time controls

Bullet is under three minutes per player (1|0, 2|1). Blitz is three to ten minutes (3|0, 3|2, 5|0). Rapid is ten to sixty minutes (10|0, 15|10). The second number is the increment: seconds added to your clock after every move you make, so you never run completely out of time in a won position.
