What is a FEN?

FEN (Forsyth–Edwards Notation) describes a chess position in one line of text. It lists the pieces rank by rank from the eighth rank to the first: capital letters for White (K Q R B N P), small letters for Black, and numbers for empty squares. After a space comes whose move it is (w or b), then castling rights, the en passant square and two move counters.

You can copy a FEN from most chess apps and websites, or set up any position on the board in BEST CHESS and copy it from there.
